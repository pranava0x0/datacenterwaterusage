Cedar Creek Lake campus (withdrawn) · Diode Ventures · Trinidad / Tool, Henderson County, Texas · withdrawn 2026-07-23

A Kansas developer proposed a data center campus west of Cedar Creek Lake in Henderson County, Texas, planned to build out over 12 years and to draw up to 5 million gallons per day of surface water from the reservoir by its fifth year. Cedar Creek Lake supplies most of Fort Worth's drinking water and was sitting below its conservation pool. More than 2,500 residents signed a Save Cedar Creek Lake petition, and the company withdrew the project in July 2026 after saying it could not meet the governor's new data-center development standards.

Amazon campus, Wharton County · Amazon Data Services · Boling, Wharton County, Texas · approved 2026-09-08

Amazon is planning a nearly 3,000-acre, $10 billion data center campus near Boling in Wharton County, Texas. The Coastal Bend Groundwater Conservation District approved its groundwater permits on September 8, 2026 — up to 78.2 million gallons over two and a half years for offices, restrooms, construction crews and fire protection. The first buildings are to be air cooled; as the campus grows Amazon expects to need about 500 acre-feet a year of surface water from the Lower Colorado River Authority for cooling on the hottest hours, and has not yet applied for it. Neighbors raised drinking-water wells, electricity costs and backup-generator pollution at the hearing.

Sources: GovTech / Houston Chronicle: Amazon clears first hurdle for $10B data center near Houston, Houston Chronicle: Amazon's Wharton County data center groundwater permits approved
